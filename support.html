Hotwire Support

Combat racing on iPhone and iPad.

Contact: support@roughgames.studio
Please include your device model, iOS version, the game version (bottom of SETTINGS) and what happened. We usually reply within 2 working days.

A purchase is missing

Open STORE and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

ONLINE doesn't connect

Check your internet connection and try again. On a restricted network (school, office, some public Wi-Fi), try mobile data. When few players are online, empty seats fill with computer racers after a few seconds, so you always get a race.

Racing with friends

ONLINE › PRIVATE ROOM creates a room and a 6-character code. Friends enter it with JOIN WITH CODE.

Someone behaved badly online

Tap the player's name in the room or the results to BLOCK or REPORT them. There is no chat in Hotwire, only emotes.

My progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. There are no accounts.
