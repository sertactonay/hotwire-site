Hotwire Privacy Policy

Effective: 21 November 2026. Applies to the Hotwire app for iPhone and iPad, including test versions on TestFlight.

In short: Hotwire has no ads and never reads your advertising ID. If you are signed in to Game Center, your progress is backed up to our cloud save service so it follows you to a new iPhone; otherwise it stays on your device. The app sends anonymous usage and crash data, tied to a random ID it creates, to Google Firebase so we can fix and improve the game. When you race online, your racer name, an anonymous player ID and your car's setup go through our networking provider so the race can run. We don't sell data or share it for advertising.

Who we are

Hotwire is made by Sertaç Tonay, an independent developer based in Türkiye ("we", "us"). Contact: support@roughgames.studio.

Data that stays on your device

  • Your progress and settings: career, credits, cars, upgrades, paints, settings, your chosen racer name, and the players you blocked or reported. They are saved in the app's own storage. If you use Game Center, a copy of your progress is also kept in cloud save (see below).
  • Purchase status: a local copy of which in-app purchases you own, so the game works offline.
  • Freeze notes: if the game freezes for several seconds, it writes a short technical note (what the game was doing, the scene, timings) into the app's own folder. It contains no personal information and is never sent automatically.

Deleting the app deletes all of this from your device; it does not delete your cloud save.

Cloud save (only when you are signed in to Game Center)

If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, Hotwire uses it to sign you in to Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication and Unity Cloud Save, provided by Unity Technologies ApS and Unity Software Inc.), which store your game progress for us as our service provider. Apple confirms your Game Center sign-in to Unity with a signed, game-specific player identifier, and Unity creates a player ID for you.

  • What we store: your progress (career, credits, cars, upgrades, paints, season progress, racer name and the players you blocked or reported), and a one-way code of your cloud player that tells accounts apart on a shared device.
  • What we don't store: your Game Center name, email, Apple Account or payment details. We never show your Game Center name to other players.
  • What stays on your device: settings, purchase status and freeze notes.

Unity processes your IP address to provide the service and may process data in the USA. Test versions on TestFlight keep progress in a separate test area of the same service. See Unity's privacy policy.

Your cloud save is kept until you delete it. You can delete it in the game under SETTINGS › CLOUD SAVE › DELETE CLOUD SAVE. This deletes the cloud copy and your Unity player account at once; progress on your device stays. If you are not signed in to Game Center, nothing is sent and your progress stays only on your device.

Usage and crash data (Google Firebase)

When you are online, the app sends the following to Google Firebase (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google LLC and Google Ireland Ltd.), which processes it for us as our service provider:

  • which screens and buttons are used, races and their results, cars and paints chosen, and purchases made (product and outcome only, never payment details);
  • crash reports and technical error counts (device model, iOS version, game version, what the game was doing);
  • a random ID that the app creates when it is installed. It is not your name, email, Apple Account, advertising ID or device name.

Firebase derives your approximate country or region from your IP address; it does not get your precise location. Advertising features, Google Signals and ad personalisation are switched off. The data may be processed in the USA. Firebase keeps event data for up to 14 months. See Firebase's privacy information.

The random ID is not linked to you, so we cannot find "your" data by your name. Deleting and reinstalling the app creates a new ID.

Online races (only when you use ONLINE)

Online races run through Photon, a multiplayer cloud service by Exit Games GmbH (Germany) and Exit Games Inc. (USA). We don't run our own servers and we don't keep online race data. While you are online, the following is sent through Photon to the other players in your race:

WhatWhy
Your racer name (the name you choose, or a default like RACER-1234)Shown to the other racers
An anonymous player ID: 8 characters computed from a random identifier the app creates. It is not your name, email, Apple Account or advertising IDTo tell racers apart, for rematches and for the block list
Your car, its paint and looks, and its upgrade levelsSo others see your car and the race stays fair
Race data: your car's position and movement, powers used, finish times, emotes, the game version and the server regionTo run the race

To connect you, Photon processes your device's IP address and uses network speed (ping) to pick the nearest server region. See Photon's Privacy & Cookie Policy.

There is no text or voice chat. If you report a player, the report is stored only on your device.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app only learns which product you bought, so it can unlock it.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no advertising ID, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No email or phone collection, no passwords, no precise location, contacts or photos. The only account is the cloud save player that Game Center sign-in creates.
  • No selling or renting of data.

Children

Hotwire is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Racer names pass a word filter, and online play has no chat.

Your rights

If you live in the EU/EEA, the UK or Türkiye (KVKK), you have rights to information, access, correction, deletion and objection, including the right to object to usage data. Write to us at support@roughgames.studio and we will help, including with any request to Google, Photon or Unity. You can delete your cloud save and Unity player account yourself in SETTINGS. You can also stop online data by not using ONLINE, or delete the app to erase everything on your device. You can complain to your local data protection authority.

Legal basis (GDPR/KVKK): online race data is processed to provide the online races you ask for (performance of a contract); usage and crash data is processed for our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and improving it; cloud save is processed to provide the backup you use by signing in to Game Center (performance of a contract).

Changes

If the game adds features that use data (for example ads or leaderboards), we will update this page and the App Store privacy details before that version is released. The date at the top shows the latest version.
